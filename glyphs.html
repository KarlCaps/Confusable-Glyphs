Confusable Glyphs

Characters that look similar but have different meanings and Unicode values.

What are Confusable Glyphs?

Confusable glyphs are characters that look identical or almost identical but have different Unicode values.

They can cause readability problems and may also create security problems on the web.

Why Does It Matter?

Confusable characters can cause readability issues, accessibility problems, and security vulnerabilities.

Users should pay attention to characters that look similar but have different Unicode values.
